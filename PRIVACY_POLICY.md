# Privacy Policy for Emoji Guess

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Welcome to **Emoji Guess** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an entertaining and engaging mobile game experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information, advertising, and user data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
Emoji Guess is designed to store game progress and settings locally on your device using Android DataStore Preferences. This includes:
- Game progress, stage levels, and high scores.
- In-game virtual currency (Coins) and streak counters.
- Audio and vibration preferences (Sound ON/OFF, Vibration ON/OFF).
- Daily challenge completion timestamps.

**This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our private servers.**

### B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services
Our app integrates third-party services that may automatically collect standard device and usage information to serve advertisements and ensure app stability:

- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: Uses advertising IDs, device identifiers, IP addresses, coarse location data, and app interaction data to deliver personalized or non-personalized banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
- **Google Play Services**: Collects standard system diagnostics and performance metrics.

For more details on how Google processes ad-related data, please visit [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. How We Use Information

The data handled by Emoji Guess is used solely for:
- Saving your game progress, high scores, and preferences locally.
- Displaying advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video Ads) provided by Google AdMob.
- Ensuring smooth gameplay, audio feedback, and haptic animations.

---

## 3. Advertising & Cookies

We use **Google AdMob** to display ads within the game. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies to:
- Serve contextual or personalized ads based on your advertising settings.
- Frequency cap ad displays so you do not see the same ad repeatedly.
- Measure ad performance and report ad interactions.

You can manage or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:
- Go to **Settings > Privacy > Ads** (or **Google > Ads**) and toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization** or **Reset advertising ID**.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Emoji Guess is a general audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

## 5. Security

Since all your game progress is saved locally using Android’s secure DataStore Preferences, your data is protected by the Android operating system's sandbox model. 

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since we do not store your data on external servers or require account registration, you can clear all stored game data at any time by:
1. Opening your device **Settings**.
2. Navigating to **Apps > Emoji Guess > Storage**.
3. Tapping **Clear Data** or **Clear Cache**.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
