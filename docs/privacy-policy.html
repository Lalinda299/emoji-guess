Privacy Policy for Emoji Guess

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 | Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Welcome to Emoji Guess. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an entertaining and engaging mobile game experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information, advertising, and user data.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Emoji Guess is designed to store game progress and settings locally on your device using Android DataStore Preferences. This includes:

  • Game progress, stage levels, and high scores.
  • In-game virtual currency (Coins) and streak counters.
  • Audio and vibration preferences (Sound ON/OFF, Vibration ON/OFF).
  • Daily challenge completion timestamps.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our private servers.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

Our app integrates third-party services that may automatically collect standard device and usage information to serve advertisements and ensure app stability:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Uses advertising IDs, device identifiers, IP addresses, coarse location data, and app interaction data to deliver personalized or non-personalized banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • Google Play Services: Collects standard system diagnostics and performance metrics.

For more details on how Google processes ad-related data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

The data handled by Emoji Guess is used solely for:

  • Saving your game progress, high scores, and preferences locally.
  • Displaying advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video Ads) provided by Google AdMob.
  • Ensuring smooth gameplay, audio feedback, and haptic animations.

3. Advertising & Cookies

We use Google AdMob to display ads within the game. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies to serve contextual or personalized ads. You can manage your advertising preferences anytime in Android Settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

4. Children’s Privacy

Emoji Guess is a general audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security & Data Deletion

Since all game progress is saved locally on your device, you can clear all stored game data at any time via Settings > Apps > Emoji Guess > Storage > Clear Data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our GitHub Repository.
